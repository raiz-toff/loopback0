## ICMP behavior across different subnet masks

The ping succeeds because devices forward traffic to their default gateways when destinations appear to be in different subnets. If the gateway correctly resolves the MAC address via ARP, the ICMP echo request and reply sequence will complete successfully.

> **Note:** This behavior highlights the importance of ARP resolution over strict subnet mask matching in point-to-point topologies.
